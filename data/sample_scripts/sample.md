# INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY

JOHN sits at a corner table, scrolling his phone. "Blinding Lights" by The Weeknd plays softly over the speakers.

He opens his laptop, a Coca-Cola can beside it. Across the room, a poster of TOM CRUISE hangs on the wall.

JOHN
(muttering)
I can't believe we're filming this at the Eiffel Tower next week.

He glances at his watch and stands up.

# INT. SPACESHIP BRIDGE - NIGHT

CAPTAIN VEX stares out at the stars, humming a tune she made up herself.

Her co-pilot ZARA sips from an unmarked metal flask and checks the nav console.

CAPTAIN VEX
(quietly)
We're three days from the Kepler outpost. Nothing out here but us and the dark.

ZARA nods, saying nothing.
